Class Demo Instructions

  1. Login and open browser DevTools - Application/Storage - Cookies.
  2. Find connect.sid and verify HttpOnly and SameSite are set.
  3. Go to Vault and click "Try to Steal Cookie"; session cookie should not be visible in document.cookie.
  4. Wait 60 seconds and refresh Vault; session should expire and redirect.
  5. (Teacher demo) Temporarily disable httpOnly in server.js, restart server, and show how scripts can now read the cookie.
